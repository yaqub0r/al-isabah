# Bounded gated successor preparation

This proposed runbook prepares one **new** 13-record successor after a separately
reviewed obligation seed and an exact user-origin decision. It neither reuses
the exhausted Sol/High decision nor authorizes a worker by itself. The saved
Sol/High three-stage history is the immutable baseline, with its partial result
and all concerns retained. The seed is a known-obligation index, not an
exhaustive source reading or a human scholarly review.

The request must bind the frozen source artifact, packet and partition, draft3
profile and output schema, final Sol/High report/output/full history and receipt
chain, reviewed seed, coverage-gate code and schema, local method, this runbook,
the committed history verifier, and the committed successor runner. It permits
at most three fresh GPT-6 Sol/High workers. Extraction may submit a candidate proposal, finer anchors,
and expanded ledger together. Do not require a complete independently reviewed
atom ledger before extraction. The gate checks known obligations for material
progress only; byte containment and seed discriminators do not establish
source entailment or exhaustive coverage.

Run extraction first. Capture its actual host metadata, complete proposal and
ledger, and deterministic Gate 1 result. Independent review may be prepared
only when that result is `partial_progress` and its seed, ledger, proposal and
first receipt digests match. The review and adjudication inputs carry the full
immutable extraction ledger and Gate 1 result, so their workers can inspect
the exact anchors and outcomes; a digest alone is insufficient review context.
Adjudication depends on the same gate binding and the reviewed predecessor
receipt. Every worker must have a fresh non-forked context, exact effective
model and reasoning, distinct session, and observed parent task. Worker IDs
from every stage in the authenticated nested baseline and Sol history are
excluded. Missing gate evidence or relabeled concerns fail closed.

Adjudication must submit a final ledger for its exact final proposal and run a
deterministic final reconciliation before its receipt is captured. It may
retain a corrected new object with supported anchors, or mark a rejected
candidate unresolved with an allowed reason. The reconciliation verifies the
full ledger against the frozen packet and reviewed seed. Only a successful
material gate check reports `retained_partial_progress`; a structurally valid
ledger with no established material progress reports `not_established`. Other
gate failures reject the report. The final report keeps extraction Gate 1
status separate from this final assessment. Neither status proves source
entailment or exhaustive coverage. Human scholarly review stays `unreviewed`;
machine review is a separate stage. Final validation never authorizes actual
consumer admission, public release or full-volume completion.

No actual successor request, decision, worker, export or admission is created
by the proposed code alone. The reviewed seed and exact authorization are
separate accountable decisions.
